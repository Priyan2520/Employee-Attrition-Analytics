## 1. Professional Resume Bullet Points
*Copy and paste these directly under your internship or project experience sections. They focus on the **Action-Context-Result** framework.*

* **Data Analysis & Modeling:** Analyzed voluntary employee attrition trends using Python and Power BI across **1,470 workforce records**, identifying overtime and compensation as primary drivers of turnover.
* **Dashboard Development:** Engineered an interactive, **5-page Power BI dashboard** featuring KPI tracking (Total Headcount, Attrition Rate %, Retention Rate %, and Salary Gaps), demographic analysis, and role-based turnover insights.
* **Data Cleaning & ETL:** Conducted data cleaning, validation, and feature engineering using **Pandas and NumPy** in Python; annualized salaries, binned age cohorts, and isolated zero-variance features to improve model performance and efficiency.
* **Business Recommendations:** Formulated **5 strategic, data-supported retention recommendations**—including overtime caps and restructuring junior compensation structures—targeting high-risk cohorts with attrition rates up to **39.8%**.
* **Project Documentation:** Documented the complete analytics lifecycle from data preparation and Python ETL pipelines to Power BI data modeling guides and executive business reporting.

## 3. Key Project Metrics (Quick Cheat-Sheet for Interviews)
When talking to recruiters, refer to these exact numbers:
* **Total Headcount:** 1,470 employees
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.1% (237 voluntary departures)
* **Overtime Attrition Impact:** 30.5% turnover for overtime staff vs. 10.4% for non-overtime staff (3x turnover risk)
* **Median Salary Gap:** $23,880 annual gap ($62,400 retained vs. $38,520 departing)
* **Highest Risk Role:** Sales Representatives (39.8% attrition rate)
* **Highest Risk Age Cohort:** 18–25 age group (36.2% attrition rate)
